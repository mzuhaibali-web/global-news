AMD Plans Major Increase in AI Chip Supply for 2027

October 6, 2026

AMD is preparing to significantly increase its supply of artificial intelligence chips in 2027 as demand for AI computing continues to expand.

Chief Executive Officer Lisa Su said the company is working with suppliers in Taiwan and South Korea to secure additional production capacity for advanced semiconductor products.

The company is also seeking access to more advanced wafer manufacturing capacity as demand for AI processors continues to grow across the technology industry.

AI chips have become an important area of competition among major semiconductor companies. AMD is competing with Nvidia as businesses and technology companies invest heavily in artificial intelligence infrastructure.

Increasing chip production could help AMD meet expected demand from customers building data centers and other systems designed to run artificial intelligence applications.

The semiconductor industry is expected to remain an important part of the global technology market as companies continue developing more powerful AI systems.
