Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Global News. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit our website or use its features.

By using this website, you acknowledge that information may be handled as described in this policy, subject to applicable law.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect information such as your name and email address when you create an account or contact us.

Basic technical information, such as browser type, device information and pages visited, may also be processed by hosting providers or third-party services used to operate the website.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Provide account and authentication features.
  • Respond to questions and contact messages.
  • Protect the website against misuse and security threats.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Support advertising, if advertising services are enabled.

4. Accounts and Authentication

If you register or sign in, information needed for authentication may be processed by the service provider used for account management, such as Supabase, if it is enabled on the relevant feature.

Please do not share your password with anyone. Information handled by an external service is also subject to that service's own policies and practices.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies or similar technologies may be used by the website or its third-party providers to support essential functionality, remember preferences, understand website usage or provide advertising.

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

6. Advertising and Google AdSense

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled on Global News, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize advertisements, where permitted by applicable law and user preferences.

Google's use of advertising cookies may allow Google and its partners to show advertisements based on visits to this website or other websites. Advertising practices and available controls may vary by location.

To learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, visit Google's partner-sites information page

If advertising is added to this website, this policy and any required consent or privacy notices will be reviewed and updated to reflect the services actually being used.

7. Third-Party Services

Global News may rely on third-party providers for hosting, authentication, website functionality, analytics or advertising. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not claim that every possible third-party service is active on this website. This policy should be updated whenever services are added, removed or materially changed.

8. Data Sharing and Retention

We do not intend to sell personal information provided directly to us. Information may be processed by service providers where needed to operate the website, respond to requests, protect security or meet legal obligations.

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. Actual retention periods may depend on the service involved.

9. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information handled by the website. However, no website, transmission method or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to some types of processing.

You may also manage cookies through your browser and use available advertising privacy controls. To make a privacy request, contact us through our Contact page.

11. Children's Privacy

Global News is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

12. International Visitors

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, applicable safeguards should be used for such processing.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as website features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact us through the Global News Contact page.

You can also read About Global News or return to our home page.
