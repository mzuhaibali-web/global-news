South Asia Growth Forecast Raised to 6.9% for 2026

October 6, 2026

The World Bank has raised its 2026 growth forecast for South Asia to 6.9%, citing resilient domestic demand and strong remittance inflows.

The regional economy has remained relatively resilient despite pressure from higher energy prices and wider global economic uncertainty.

India, the region's largest economy, is projected to maintain strong growth during the 2026/27 period. Domestic demand is expected to remain an important contributor to economic activity.

The World Bank has also pointed to artificial intelligence as a potential opportunity for South Asian economies. Greater use of AI could support productivity and create new opportunities for businesses and workers.

However, the region still faces several risks. Higher energy costs, severe weather events and changes in global financial markets could affect economic growth in the future.

Economists and policymakers are therefore expected to continue monitoring global conditions while focusing on investment, employment and long-term economic growth.

Source: Reuters / World Bank
