Uzbekistan Investment Opportunities: Reforms & Key Sectors

October 6, 2026

By Global News Editorial Team

Economic Reforms in Uzbekistan

Uzbekistan is continuing to attract international investors as economic reforms introduced in recent years reshape the country's business environment.

The country has made significant changes since 2016, with reforms aimed at increasing private-sector activity, improving investment conditions and reducing the role of the state in parts of the economy.

Key Investment Sectors in Uzbekistan

Uzbekistan is seeking to attract investment across several parts of its economy. Manufacturing, energy, mining, agriculture and transport are among the sectors investors may examine when assessing business opportunities in the country.

Manufacturing and Industrial Development

Industrial development and local manufacturing are important parts of Uzbekistan's economic reform agenda. Opportunities may include processing raw materials, producing industrial goods and developing supply chains for domestic and regional markets.

Renewable Energy and Electricity

Energy development is another area attracting attention. Solar and wind projects, electricity generation and improvements to energy infrastructure may offer opportunities for companies with relevant technical and financial expertise.

Mining and Mineral Resources

Uzbekistan has a significant mining industry. Mineral extraction, processing technology and related infrastructure are areas international companies may assess, subject to local regulations and project requirements.

Agriculture and Food Processing

Agriculture remains an important part of the economy. Investment in modern farming methods, food processing, storage and agricultural logistics could support productivity and access to wider markets.

Transport and Logistics

Uzbekistan's position in Central Asia makes transport links and logistics infrastructure relevant to regional trade. Warehousing, freight services and transport connectivity are areas businesses may consider as regional trade develops.

Foreign Investment and Economic Growth

According to UNCTAD's World Investment Report 2026, inward foreign direct investment (FDI) flows were approximately $2.275 billion in 2021 and an estimated $4.398 billion in 2025. These figures represent annual investment flows and do not guarantee future investment growth.

According to the World Bank, Uzbekistan recorded average annual real GDP growth of approximately 6 percent between 2017 and 2025. Its economy grew by 7.7 percent in 2025. Banking, energy and mining remain important sectors where state-owned companies continue to have a major presence.

At the same time, businesses still face challenges including bureaucratic delays and shortages of skilled workers. Further reforms could help improve the investment environment and support private businesses.

The country's continued economic reforms are being closely watched by international investors as Uzbekistan seeks to expand its role in regional and global markets.
