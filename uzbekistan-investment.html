Uzbekistan Attracts Investors as Economic Reforms Continue

October 6, 2026

By Global News Editorial Team

Economic Reforms in Uzbekistan

Uzbekistan is continuing to attract international investors as economic reforms introduced in recent years reshape the country's business environment.

The country has made significant changes since 2016, with reforms aimed at increasing private-sector activity, improving investment conditions and reducing the role of the state in parts of the economy.

Foreign Investment and Economic Growth

Foreign direct investment has increased from around $2.3 billion in 2021 to approximately $4.4 billion in 2025. The increase reflects growing interest from international companies and investors looking at opportunities in Uzbekistan.

Uzbekistan's economy has also maintained average growth of about 6 percent over the past five years. Banking, energy and mining remain important sectors where state-owned companies continue to have a major presence.

At the same time, businesses still face challenges including bureaucratic delays and shortages of skilled workers. Further reforms could help improve the investment environment and support private businesses.

The country's continued economic reforms are being closely watched by international investors as Uzbekistan seeks to expand its role in regional and global markets.
